# Extended Industrialization

<MarginFloatingImage src="assets/logo.png" left="0" right="10" />

Extended Industrialization is an addon for Modern Industrialization that adds a variety of machines and tools!

The primary goal of Extended Industrialization is to extend the content of Modern Industrialization to cover more ground
while also staying true to its design and intended use. For example, the addition of
[batching multiblocks](machines/batching_multiblocks.md) allows for scaling factories even further than before while
needing to configure less machines and saving on performance.
